## Cash flow of purchase consideration

`Gross consideration/equity value = cash at closing + escrow withheld + upfront rollover value + expected or maximum contingent earnout **according to explicit valuation convention**`.

Illustration: R$153.00 all-in maximum nominal consideration could be R$123.0 paid now, R$15.0 escrow withholding, R$15.0 max earnout, *if these are agreed components* (sum = 153); seller cash today **123**, not 153; earnout discounted expected PV usually differs from cap nominal. If the R$153 is expected PV, solve upfront allocation accordingly; do not silently mix nominal max and PV.
