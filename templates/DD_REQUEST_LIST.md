# Due diligence request list — fictional target

In future simulated VDR each item receives `request_id`, workstream, materiality, due date, version, evidence hash, permission class, owner and reviewed/accepted status. Everything synthetic.

| Workstream | Requested information | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Finance | 3–5 years audited/management DRE, BP and DFC | revenue/earnings/cash history |
| Finance | Monthly trial balance and GL, 24–36 months | QoE and seasonality |
| Finance | Revenue bridge by service/subscription, invoice and contract | recurrence and realization |
| Finance | AR aging, writeoffs, receipts after period-end | working capital and recoverability |
| Finance | AP aging, accrued costs, payroll accruals | NWC/debt-like reconciliation |
| Finance | Bank statements and treasury, cash restrictions | true cash at closing |
| Finance | Loans, overdrafts, lease contracts, covenant schedule | debt and debt-like definitions |
| Finance | Asset register, capitalized development and amortization | cash capex vs EBITDA consistency |
| Finance | Dividend/shareholder loans and related party transactions | cash leakages / equity price |
| Finance | EBITDA normalization evidence, 1-off invoices | recurrence, management adjustment proof |
| Tax | Corporate tax returns, audits, payment schedule | litigation/exposure |
| Tax | Disputed liabilities with legal status and probability | contingent tax risk |
| Legal | Shareholder register, bylaws and cap table | ownership/consent |
| Legal | Contracts with change-of-control clauses | transaction feasibility |
| Legal | Litigation register and provisions | risk and debt-like |
| IP/IT | Software IP assignments and third-party licenses | intellectual property ownership |
| IP/IT | Architecture/security incidents/dependencies | integration and security DD |
| Commercial | Top 20 customers sales, retention, concentration | client/dependency risk |
| Commercial | Contract renewal/churn and pricing policy | forecast reliability |
| Commercial | Pipeline conversion and cancellations | future sales forecasts |
| HR | Key roles, founder agreements, turnover, accrued obligations | succession and debt-like |
| Operations | Business continuity, service SLA breaches | delivery risk |
| Regulatory | Licenses/competition screening and counsel opinions | regulatory gates |
| Privacy | Data processing register and access controls | LGPD/security planning |

**Red flag resolution:** each issue gets one treatment among forecast adjustment, price bridge, escrow/indemnity, closing condition, or separate explanation. Multiple treatments require reasoned no-double-count validation.
