# Investment banking deliverables and presentation specifications

Every exported file needs report ID, version, valuation date, source run ID, `SYNTHETIC — portfolio illustration`, author/reviewer and assumptions footnotes. Use English transaction acronyms with Portuguese explanations.

## 1. Financial model workbook

See `FINANCIAL_MODEL.md` for 33-tab contract. Include 'checks' tab and charts printable. Key outputs: normalized EBITDA, 5-year forecast, DCF, terminal value and sensitivities, public trading comps, standalone-to-synergy separation, EV/equity price bridge, NWC/earnout/escrow.

## 2. Sell-side anonymous teaser (one page)

No identifying fictitious company name in buyer-facing teaser. Introduce "Projeto Aurora" only; investment highlights, sector, scale, strategic rationale, 3 selected KPIs, sales process/disclosure disclaimer. No fake actual market claims.

## 3. Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM, 12–18 slides/pages)

Cover/confidentiality; executive summary; investment highlights; industry; company overview; business model; recurring revenue/customer mix; commercial/operating KPIs; management; financial history; QoE caveats; projections and assumptions; investment opportunities/risks; growth initiatives; transaction perimeter/process; source appendix and synthetic disclaimer. Source every metric; never label pro forma as audited.

## 4. Management presentation (8–12 slides)

Equity story; problem/solution; products; customer concentration and mitigations; unit economics; operations; financial progress and business plan; growth levers; execution risks; Q&A. If founders are fictitious, do not create real-looking portraits.

## 5. Investment committee memo (3–6 pages)

Recommendation (Proceed / Renegotiate / Walk Away), transaction rationale, valuation range vs bid, funding/financing, synergy *separate* from standalone, deal economics, DD findings, scenario sensitivities, negotiation plan, investment risks, open conditions and explicit approval request. References must include source IDs and run ID. The recommendation can be `conditional / not actionable` when sources are insufficient.

## 6. Financial due diligence report (5–10 pages)

QoE bridge (reported vs adjusted EBITDA); recurring earnings; cash conversion; working capital peg; debt-like items; customer concentration; forecasting reliability; quantitative and qualitative risk items; price impact and negotiation questions.

## 7. Buyer screening and bids

A fictional longlist, investment style/strategic fit, scorecard, process statuses, IOI/LOI matrix including headline EV, equity bridge, cash upfront, contingent earn-out, financing risk, exclusivity, conditions, expected close. Need qualitative deal quality, not largest headline EV alone.

## 8. Simulated closing pack

Exhibit A: closing price mechanics and cash/debt/working capital definitions. Exhibit B: hypothetical funds flow. Exhibit C: escrow/earnout economic schedule. Exhibit D: condition satisfaction log and integration 100-day plan. All are financial training templates and NOT legally binding documents.

## Output checklist

- Numerical parity across workbook/dashboard/PPT/report.
- No private company invented as public comp; public figures sourced.
- No leakage of target identity in anonymous teaser.
- Present downside/uncertainty (not just bullish investment highlights).
- Editable source files and reproducible generation instructions.
- Audience appropriate: partner/IC can reach a decision from 2-page summary.
